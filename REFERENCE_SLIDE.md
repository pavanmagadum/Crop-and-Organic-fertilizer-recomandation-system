### 📊 Datasets & Open Sources
* **Kaggle Agricultural Datasets:** Primary source for N-P-K soil nutrient data and crop classification features.
* **Open Government Data (OGD) Platform India:** (data.gov.in) Used for regional climate patterns and agricultural statistics.
* **FAOSTAT:** Food and Agriculture Organization of the United Nations (FAO) database for global crop trends.
* **ICAR - Indian Council of Agricultural Research:** Reference for soil pH standards and fertilizer mapping logic.

---

### 🛠️ Frameworks & Libraries
* **Streamlit (v1.32+):** Used for building the interactive glassmorphism UI.
* **Scikit-Learn:** Core library for the Random Forest classification models.
* **Pandas & NumPy:** Essential tools for data manipulation and synthetic data generation.
* **Joblib:** Used for efficient model serialization and deployment.
* **OpenWeatherMap API:** Real-time weather integration for local farming insights.
